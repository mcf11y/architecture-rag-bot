# The Sith

The Sith were an ancient order of Force-users who embraced the dark side and stood as the eternal enemies of the Jedi. Driven by ambition, hatred, and the desire for power, the Sith sought to dominate the galaxy and destroy the Jedi Order that opposed them.

For much of their later history, the Sith followed a strict rule that allowed only two to exist at any time: a master to embody power, and an apprentice to crave it. This rule of two kept their numbers hidden and their ambitions secret for a thousand years, as they patiently plotted revenge against the Jedi.

The Sith achieved their greatest triumph under Darth Sidious, who concealed his identity as the politician Palpatine. Working with apprentices such as Darth Maul and later Darth Vader, he orchestrated the Clone Wars, destroyed the Jedi Order through Order 66, and founded the Galactic Empire. The Sith wielded lightsabers, typically with red blades, and drew their strength from raw emotion and the dark side of the Force.
