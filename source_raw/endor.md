# Endor

Endor was a forested moon covered in towering ancient trees, gentle meadows, and abundant wildlife. Its temperate climate and lush greenery made it a haven for life, including its native inhabitants, the small furry Ewoks who lived in villages built high among the treetops.

The moon gained galactic significance when the Galactic Empire chose to construct a second, far larger Death Star in orbit above it. To protect the unfinished battle station, the Empire built a powerful shield generator on the moon's surface, defended by a garrison of stormtroopers.

Endor became the site of the decisive Battle of Endor. A Rebel Alliance strike team, aided by the Ewoks, landed on the moon to destroy the shield generator, while the Rebel fleet engaged the Empire in orbit. The primitive but determined Ewoks helped defeat the Imperial forces on the ground, allowing the Rebels to destroy the second Death Star and the Emperor along with it.
