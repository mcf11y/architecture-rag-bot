# C-3PO

C-3PO was a protocol droid fluent in over six million forms of communication, built to assist with etiquette, translation, and diplomacy. Golden-plated and perpetually anxious, he was known for his fussy manners and his constant worrying, which often provided comic relief amid danger.

C-3PO was originally constructed by a young Anakin Skywalker on Tatooine. Over the years the droid passed through many owners, eventually serving Padmé Amidala and later the Rebel Alliance. He was almost always accompanied by his counterpart, the astromech droid R2-D2, with whom he shared countless adventures.

Though timid and easily flustered, C-3PO proved surprisingly important to the Rebellion. On the forest moon of Endor, the native Ewoks mistook the golden droid for a deity, and C-3PO persuaded them to join the fight against the Galactic Empire. His fluency in language repeatedly helped his friends survive hostile encounters across the galaxy.
