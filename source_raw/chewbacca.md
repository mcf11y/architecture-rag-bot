# Chewbacca

Chewbacca was a Wookiee warrior from the forest world of Kashyyyk and the loyal co-pilot and closest friend of Han Solo. Standing more than two meters tall and covered in shaggy brown fur, he was immensely strong, fiercely loyal, and skilled with a bowcaster.

Chewbacca owed Han a life debt, a sacred Wookiee bond that kept the two companions together for decades aboard the Millennium Falcon. Though he spoke only in growls and roars that few outsiders could understand, Chewie was a brilliant mechanic who kept the ship's aging hyperdrive running through countless narrow escapes.

He fought alongside the Rebel Alliance in many battles, including the Battle of Endor, where the Wookiee helped the Rebels bring down the shield protecting the second Death Star. Chewbacca remained at Han Solo's side through smuggling runs, wars, and adventures, a symbol of unwavering friendship and courage.
