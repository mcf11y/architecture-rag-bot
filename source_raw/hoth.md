# Hoth

Hoth was a remote and frozen world covered entirely in ice and snow, swept by ferocious blizzards and plunging night temperatures that could kill an unprotected traveler within hours. Its barren surface was home to few creatures, among them the predatory wampa and the herd-dwelling tauntaun.

Because of its isolation and hostile climate, Hoth was chosen by the Rebel Alliance as the site of a hidden base after they were forced to abandon their previous stronghold. Deep within the ice, the Rebels built Echo Base, a network of tunnels and hangars from which they could operate beyond the reach of the Galactic Empire.

The planet became the site of one of the war's most famous engagements, the Battle of Hoth. When the Empire discovered the hidden base, it launched a massive ground assault led by towering armored walkers. The Rebels were overwhelmed and forced to flee, but their escape allowed the Rebellion to survive and fight on.
