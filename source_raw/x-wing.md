# The X-wing

The X-wing was a versatile single-pilot starfighter that became the backbone of the Rebel Alliance's fleet. Named for the distinctive way its four wings split into an X-shape during combat, it balanced speed, firepower, and durability better than almost any other fighter of its class.

Each X-wing carried powerful laser cannons and proton torpedoes, and was equipped with a socket for an astromech droid, such as R2-D2, that managed repairs and navigation in flight. Its hyperdrive allowed pilots to travel between star systems, making it capable of both defense and long-range strikes.

The X-wing earned its place in history at the Battle of Yavin, where a small squadron of the fighters attacked the Death Star. Flying an X-wing with R2-D2 in the socket behind him, Luke Skywalker trusted the Force to guide his aim and fired the shot that destroyed the battle station. The fighter went on to serve the Rebellion in countless battles against the Galactic Empire.
