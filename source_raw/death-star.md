# The Death Star

The Death Star was a moon-sized battle station and the most feared superweapon ever built by the Galactic Empire. Bristling with weaponry and crewed by legions of personnel, its primary weapon was a colossal superlaser capable of destroying an entire planet with a single blast.

The station was the centerpiece of Grand Moff Tarkin's doctrine of ruling the galaxy through terror. To demonstrate its power, the Empire used the Death Star to obliterate the peaceful world of Alderaan, killing billions. The atrocity was meant to crush all resistance, but instead it galvanized the Rebel Alliance.

The Rebels obtained the stolen Death Star plans, hidden aboard the droid R2-D2, and discovered a fatal flaw: a small thermal exhaust port that led directly to the reactor. At the Battle of Yavin, Luke Skywalker used the Force to guide a single proton torpedo into the port, destroying the station. The Empire later built a second, larger Death Star, which was destroyed at the Battle of Endor.
