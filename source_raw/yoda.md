# Yoda

Yoda was one of the oldest and wisest members of the Jedi Order, serving as Grand Master of the Jedi Council for centuries. Small in stature but immensely powerful in the Force, he trained countless Jedi over the course of nearly nine hundred years.

Known for his unusual manner of speech and his deep humility, Yoda was a formidable warrior despite his age, wielding a short lightsaber with astonishing agility. During the Clone Wars he commanded armies of clone troopers on behalf of the Galactic Republic. When the Jedi Order was betrayed and destroyed under Order 66, Yoda survived and went into exile.

He hid on the remote swamp world of Dagobah, concealing himself from the Galactic Empire and the Emperor, Darth Sidious. There he later trained Luke Skywalker in the ways of the Force, teaching him to resist the temptations of the dark side. Yoda died peacefully of old age, becoming one with the Force.
