# Luke Skywalker

Luke Skywalker was a farm boy from the desert world of Tatooine who became one of the most renowned Jedi in the galaxy. Raised by his aunt and uncle, he dreamed of leaving his home planet until he encountered two runaway droids, R2-D2 and C-3PO, that carried a hidden message from Princess Leia.

Under the guidance of the aging hermit Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke began to learn the ways of the Force. He joined the Rebel Alliance and became a hero when he destroyed the Death Star during the Battle of Yavin, firing the shot that exploited the station's single weakness.

Later, Luke sought out the ancient master Yoda on the swamp world of Dagobah to complete his training as a Jedi. He eventually confronted Darth Vader and learned that the Sith Lord was his own father, Anakin Skywalker. Refusing to give in to the dark side, Luke helped redeem his father and bring balance to the Force. In his final years he became a legendary teacher, rebuilding the Jedi Order.
