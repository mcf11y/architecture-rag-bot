# The Wookiees

The Wookiees were a species of tall, powerful, fur-covered beings native to the forest world of Kashyyyk. Standing well over two meters, they possessed immense physical strength, sharp claws used for climbing, and a fierce loyalty that made them both formidable enemies and devoted friends.

Despite their intimidating appearance and short tempers, Wookiees were an intelligent and deeply honorable people. They were gifted engineers and technicians, capable of repairing complex machinery, and they communicated through a language of growls and roars that few outsiders could speak. Central to their culture was the life debt, a sacred vow of lifelong loyalty owed to one who saved a Wookiee's life.

Under the Galactic Empire, the Wookiees suffered terribly, with many enslaved and forced to labor for the regime. The most renowned member of the species was Chewbacca, who bound himself by life debt to Han Solo and served aboard the Millennium Falcon, fighting for the Rebel Alliance throughout the war.
