# The Star Destroyer

The Star Destroyer was a massive wedge-shaped warship that served as the primary symbol of the Galactic Empire's military might. Kilometers long and bristling with turbolasers, it could dominate entire star systems and strike terror into any world that considered defying Imperial rule.

Each Star Destroyer carried thousands of crew, legions of stormtroopers, squadrons of TIE fighters, and ground assault vehicles, allowing it to project power both in space and on planetary surfaces. Its sheer size and firepower made it a mobile fortress capable of blockading planets and crushing resistance.

Fleets of Star Destroyers enforced the Emperor's will across the galaxy, hunting the Rebel Alliance from world to world. It was a Star Destroyer that first captured Princess Leia's ship as she fled with the stolen Death Star plans. These warships remained the iron backbone of the Empire until its defeat at the Battle of Endor and the long collapse that followed.
