# Kashyyyk

Kashyyyk was a lush forest world dominated by colossal wroshyr trees that towered kilometers into the sky, their vast branches supporting entire cities. It was the lush and verdant home planet of the Wookiees, a species of tall, powerful, fur-covered warriors.

The Wookiees of Kashyyyk were renowned for their strength, loyalty, and skill as engineers and climbers. They lived in elevated cities built among the great trees, high above the dangerous creatures that prowled the forest floor. Their society valued honor and the sacred bond of the life debt.

During the Clone Wars, Kashyyyk was the site of a major battle, defended by Wookiee warriors alongside clone troopers and the Jedi Master Yoda. Later, under the Galactic Empire, the planet suffered greatly as many Wookiees were enslaved and forced into labor. The most famous Wookiee, Chewbacca, hailed from Kashyyyk before joining Han Solo aboard the Millennium Falcon.
