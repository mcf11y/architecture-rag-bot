# Coruscant

Coruscant was a densely populated city-world at the very heart of the galaxy, its entire surface covered by towering skyscrapers and endless layers of urban sprawl. For thousands of years it served as the political capital, first of the Galactic Republic and later of the Galactic Empire.

The planet was home to the Galactic Senate, where representatives from countless star systems gathered to debate and govern. It also housed the great temple of the Jedi Order, the training ground and headquarters of the Jedi for generations. Beneath its glittering upper levels lay a shadowy underworld of crime and poverty.

Coruscant was the stage for many pivotal events, including the political maneuvering of Chancellor Palpatine as he transformed the Republic into the Empire. When Palpatine issued Order 66 and declared himself Emperor, it was on Coruscant that the new regime centered its power, ruling the galaxy from the same halls that had once been dedicated to democracy.
