# Leia Organa

Princess Leia Organa was a political leader, diplomat, and one of the founding commanders of the Rebel Alliance. Raised as the adopted daughter of the royal family of Alderaan, she served in the Galactic Senate while secretly working against the Galactic Empire.

Captured aboard a Star Destroyer while carrying the stolen Death Star plans, Leia hid the schematics inside the droid R2-D2 before being taken prisoner by Darth Vader. She was rescued from the Death Star by Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, and Chewbacca. Her home planet of Alderaan was destroyed by the Death Star on the orders of Grand Moff Tarkin, a tragedy that hardened her resolve.

Leia was a natural leader who commanded troops during the Battle of Hoth and the Battle of Endor. She was later revealed to be the twin sister of Luke Skywalker and the daughter of Padmé Amidala and Anakin Skywalker, making her strong in the Force. She married Han Solo after the fall of the Empire.
