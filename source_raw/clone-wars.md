# The Clone Wars

The Clone Wars were a galaxy-spanning conflict fought between the Galactic Republic and the Separatists, a confederacy of worlds and corporations that sought to break away from central rule. The war raged for years across countless planets and reshaped the galaxy forever.

The Republic fought with a vast army of clone troopers, grown on the ocean world of Kamino and led into battle by Jedi generals such as Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, Mace Windu, and Yoda. The Separatists, backed by the Trade Federation, fielded enormous armies of battle droids. Neither side realized that the entire war had been secretly engineered by the Sith Lord Darth Sidious.

The Clone Wars served as the perfect cover for Palpatine's rise to power. As the fighting dragged on, he accumulated emergency authority, eroded the Jedi Order's influence, and prepared his final move. The war ended when he issued Order 66, turning the clone troopers against the Jedi and clearing the path for the birth of the Galactic Empire.
