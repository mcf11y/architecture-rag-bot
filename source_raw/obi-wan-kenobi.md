# Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi was a legendary Jedi Master, celebrated for his wisdom, his skill with a lightsaber, and his mastery of the Force. As a young Padawan he was trained by Qui-Gon Jinn, and after his master's death he took on the training of Anakin Skywalker.

During the Clone Wars, Obi-Wan served as a general of the Galactic Republic, leading clone troopers into battle across many worlds. He was a member of the Jedi Council and one of the order's most respected negotiators. His greatest sorrow came when Anakin fell to the dark side; the two fought a devastating duel on the volcanic world of Mustafar.

After the rise of the Galactic Empire and the near-destruction of the Jedi Order, Obi-Wan went into hiding on Tatooine, watching over the young Luke Skywalker. Years later he emerged to guide Luke, sacrificing himself in a duel against Darth Vader aboard the Death Star so that his allies could escape. He continued to advise Luke as a spirit within the Force.
