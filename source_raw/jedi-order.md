# The Jedi Order

The Jedi Order was an ancient monastic organization of peacekeepers, guardians, and scholars devoted to studying and serving the light side of the Force. For thousands of years the Jedi acted as protectors of the Galactic Republic, mediating disputes and defending the weak.

Jedi were identified as young children and brought to the great temple on Coruscant, where they trained for years under experienced masters. A trainee, known as a Padawan, studied under a single master until deemed ready to become a Jedi Knight. The most accomplished and wise among them earned a place on the Jedi Council, led for a time by the venerable Yoda.

The Jedi followed a strict code that warned against fear, anger, and attachment, believing these emotions led to the dark side. Their signature weapon was the lightsaber. The order was nearly destroyed when Chancellor Palpatine, secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, issued Order 66. Only a handful of Jedi, including Yoda and Obi-Wan Kenobi, survived to keep the tradition alive.
