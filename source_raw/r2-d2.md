# R2-D2

R2-D2 was a courageous and resourceful astromech droid who played a pivotal role in the greatest events of the galaxy. Small, dome-topped, and communicating only in beeps and whistles, the droid served loyally alongside heroes across several generations.

R2-D2 accompanied Anakin Skywalker as a fighter pilot's droid during the Clone Wars, and later served Padmé Amidala. The droid was famously entrusted with the stolen Death Star plans by Princess Leia, carrying the schematics to Tatooine and setting in motion the events that led to the Rebel Alliance's first great victory.

Paired almost constantly with the protocol droid C-3PO, R2-D2 repeatedly saved his companions through daring repairs, computer intrusions, and quick thinking. He served Luke Skywalker as an X-wing astromech and remained a steadfast ally of the Rebellion throughout the war against the Galactic Empire. Few droids ever influenced galactic history so profoundly.
