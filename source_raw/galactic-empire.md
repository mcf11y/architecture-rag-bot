# The Galactic Empire

The Galactic Empire was a vast authoritarian regime that ruled the galaxy through fear, military might, and absolute central control. It rose from the ashes of the Galactic Republic when Chancellor Palpatine declared himself Emperor at the end of the Clone Wars.

The Empire maintained order through an enormous military, including fleets of Star Destroyers, legions of stormtroopers, and terrifying superweapons such as the Death Star. It dissolved the Galactic Senate, stripped away civil liberties, and enslaved or oppressed countless worlds and species, including the Wookiees of Kashyyyk.

At the top of the Empire stood the Emperor, secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, served by his enforcer Darth Vader. The regime's brutality inspired widespread resistance, giving rise to the Rebel Alliance. After years of war, the Empire suffered a fatal blow at the Battle of Endor, where the second Death Star was destroyed and the Emperor was killed, beginning the collapse of Imperial rule.
