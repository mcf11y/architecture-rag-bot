# Naboo

Naboo was a lush and beautiful world of rolling green hills, shimmering lakes, and elegant classical cities. It was home to two societies that shared the planet: the human inhabitants who lived on the surface, and the amphibious Gungans who dwelt in underwater cities beneath its lakes.

Naboo was the home planet of Padmé Amidala, who served as its elected Queen and later as its senator, and also of Sheev Palpatine, who represented it in the Galactic Senate before rising to become Emperor. The planet was known for its peaceful traditions and its skilled artisans.

Naboo suffered a major crisis when the Trade Federation blockaded and invaded it in a dispute over taxation and trade. The young Queen Amidala forged an alliance with the Gungans and, with the help of the Jedi, drove out the invaders. This conflict marked the return of the Sith and the first steps in Palpatine's long rise to absolute power.
