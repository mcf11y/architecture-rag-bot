# Qui-Gon Jinn

Qui-Gon Jinn was a wise and independent-minded Jedi Master known for trusting his instincts and following what he called the living Force. Though highly respected, he often clashed with the Jedi Council, preferring to act on his own judgment rather than strict tradition.

Qui-Gon was the master of Obi-Wan Kenobi, whom he trained into a capable Jedi Knight. While on a mission to protect Padmé Amidala during the Trade Federation's blockade of Naboo, Qui-Gon discovered a gifted young slave on Tatooine named Anakin Skywalker. Convinced that the boy was the chosen one destined to bring balance to the Force, he insisted the Jedi Order train him.

Qui-Gon was slain in a duel by the Sith apprentice Darth Maul on Naboo, becoming one of the first casualties in the resurgence of the Sith. With his dying words he asked Obi-Wan to train Anakin. Qui-Gon later became one of the first Jedi to learn how to preserve his consciousness within the Force after death.
