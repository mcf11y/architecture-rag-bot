# Lando Calrissian

Lando Calrissian was a smooth-talking gambler, entrepreneur, and former smuggler who became an unlikely hero of the Rebel Alliance. Charming and resourceful, he once owned the Millennium Falcon before losing the ship to Han Solo in a game of chance.

Lando eventually settled down as the administrator of Cloud City, a mining colony floating in the skies of the gas planet Bespin. When Darth Vader arrived and forced him to betray his old friend Han Solo, Lando reluctantly cooperated, only to turn against the Galactic Empire when he saw Han frozen in carbonite and handed to the bounty hunter Boba Fett.

Redeeming himself, Lando helped rescue his friends and joined the Rebellion as a general. He led the starfighter assault on the second Death Star during the Battle of Endor, piloting the Millennium Falcon into the heart of the battle station and helping to destroy it, striking a decisive blow against the Emperor.
