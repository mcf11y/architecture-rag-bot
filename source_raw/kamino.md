# Kamino

Kamino was a remote ocean world beyond the edge of the known galaxy, its surface entirely covered by vast, storm-swept seas. Its cities were built on tall stilts rising above the endless waves, and its tall, graceful inhabitants were masters of an advanced science: cloning.

The Kaminoans were the finest cloners in the galaxy, and it was on this hidden world that a massive secret army was grown. Using the bounty hunter Jango Fett as a genetic template, they produced legions of clone troopers, bred and trained from birth to serve as soldiers.

This clone army was secretly commissioned in the name of the Galactic Republic, though the true architect of the scheme was the Sith Lord Darth Sidious. The clones formed the backbone of the Republic's forces during the Clone Wars, and were later turned against their own Jedi commanders through Order 66, becoming the first soldiers of the Galactic Empire.
