# The Lightsaber

The lightsaber was the iconic weapon of both the Jedi and the Sith, a blade of pure energy projected from an elegant handheld hilt. Capable of cutting through nearly any material and of deflecting blaster bolts, it was described as an elegant weapon for a more civilized age.

Constructing a lightsaber was a rite of passage for a Jedi. At the heart of each weapon lay a special crystal that focused the energy blade and determined its color. Jedi commonly wielded blue or green blades, while the Sith favored red crystals, corrupted through the dark side of the Force.

Mastery of the lightsaber required deep training and a strong connection to the Force, allowing a warrior to anticipate attacks and move with superhuman speed. Legendary duels defined galactic history, from Obi-Wan Kenobi's battle with Darth Maul, to his fateful duel with Anakin Skywalker on Mustafar, to Luke Skywalker's confrontation with Darth Vader. The weapon remained a symbol of the ancient orders that wielded it.
