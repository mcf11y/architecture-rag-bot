# The Hutts

The Hutts were a species of large, sluglike beings notorious throughout the galaxy for their dominance of organized crime. Slow-moving and long-lived, they possessed no legs, dragging their massive bodies along the ground, yet they wielded enormous influence through wealth, intimidation, and vast criminal networks.

Hutts were cunning, greedy, and utterly without mercy, ruling over empires of smuggling, gambling, slavery, and bounty hunting. They cared little for the wars between the Galactic Republic, the Separatists, or the Galactic Empire, concerned only with profit and the expansion of their own power. Many operated from strongholds in the lawless Outer Rim.

The most infamous Hutt was Jabba the Hutt, a powerful crime lord who ruled the underworld of Tatooine from a fortified desert palace. He employed smugglers such as Han Solo and bounty hunters such as Boba Fett, and displayed the carbonite-frozen Han as a trophy until Luke Skywalker and his allies destroyed the crime lord's operation.
