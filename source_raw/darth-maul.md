# Darth Maul

Darth Maul was a fearsome Sith apprentice trained in secret by Darth Sidious to help restore the power of the Sith and destroy the Jedi Order. Marked by red and black tattoos across his entire body, he was a silent and relentless warrior driven by rage and the dark side of the Force.

Maul's signature weapon was a double-bladed lightsaber, which he wielded with acrobatic ferocity. He was dispatched by his master to hunt down Padmé Amidala and the Jedi protecting her during the crisis on Naboo. There he fought and killed Qui-Gon Jinn in a fierce duel.

Moments later, Maul was struck down by Obi-Wan Kenobi and fell into a deep shaft, though he survived against all odds. Consumed by hatred and a thirst for revenge against Kenobi, he rebuilt himself and rose to power in the criminal underworld during the years of the Clone Wars. He remained one of the most dangerous enemies the Jedi ever faced.
