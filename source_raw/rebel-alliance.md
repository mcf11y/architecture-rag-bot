# The Rebel Alliance

The Rebel Alliance was a coalition of freedom fighters, defectors, and oppressed worlds united in armed resistance against the Galactic Empire. Formed in secret by senators and idealists who refused to accept tyranny, it fought to restore freedom and democracy to the galaxy.

Though vastly outnumbered and outgunned, the Rebellion relied on daring raids, guerrilla tactics, and the courage of volunteers. Its heroes included Princess Leia Organa, the smuggler Han Solo, and the young Jedi Luke Skywalker, whose destruction of the Death Star at the Battle of Yavin gave the Alliance its first great victory.

The Rebel Alliance endured hardship and defeat, including the loss of its hidden base during the Battle of Hoth. Yet it persevered, striking its decisive blow at the Battle of Endor, where Rebel forces destroyed the second Death Star and killed the Emperor. This triumph shattered the Empire's grip on the galaxy and paved the way for a new era of freedom.
