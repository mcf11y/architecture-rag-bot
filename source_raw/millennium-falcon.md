# The Millennium Falcon

The Millennium Falcon was a battered but legendary freighter, widely considered the fastest ship of its kind in the galaxy. Though it looked like a worn-out piece of junk from the outside, it had been heavily and illegally modified to outrun almost anything that pursued it.

The ship was famous for its lightning-quick hyperdrive, its powerful engines, and its ability to slip away from danger at the last possible moment. It was originally won and lost in games of chance, passing from Lando Calrissian into the hands of the smuggler Han Solo, who flew it with his Wookiee co-pilot Chewbacca.

The Millennium Falcon played a decisive role in the war against the Galactic Empire. It carried Luke Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Princess Leia on their earliest adventures, and it flew in the Battle of Yavin. At the Battle of Endor, Lando Calrissian piloted the Falcon into the heart of the second Death Star and fired the shot that destroyed its reactor.
