# Padmé Amidala

Padmé Amidala was a courageous stateswoman who served as Queen and later senator of Naboo. Elected to the throne as a young woman, she defended her home planet during a blockade and invasion by the Trade Federation, forming an alliance with the native Gungans to reclaim her capital.

As a senator in the Galactic Senate, Padmé was a leading voice against corruption and against granting unchecked emergency powers to Chancellor Palpatine during the Clone Wars. She secretly married the Jedi Anakin Skywalker, a forbidden union that she kept hidden from the Jedi Order.

Padmé became pregnant with twins, but her marriage suffered as Anakin was drawn toward the dark side of the Force. Heartbroken by his transformation into Darth Vader, she died shortly after giving birth to Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa. Her children would grow up to become central figures in the Rebel Alliance and the fall of the Galactic Empire.
