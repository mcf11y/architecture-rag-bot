# Jabba the Hutt

Jabba the Hutt was a powerful crime lord who ruled a vast criminal empire from his palace on the desert world of Tatooine. A member of the sluglike Hutt species, he was enormous, slow-moving, and utterly ruthless, controlling smuggling, gambling, and bounty hunting across the Outer Rim.

Jabba employed a wide network of smugglers, including Han Solo, who owed him a large debt after being forced to dump a shipment of cargo. When Han failed to pay, Jabba placed a bounty on his head, which was eventually collected by the bounty hunter Boba Fett. Jabba displayed the carbonite-frozen smuggler as a trophy in his throne room.

The crime lord met his end when Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, and their allies infiltrated his palace to rescue Han Solo. During the escape, Leia strangled Jabba, ending his reign over the Tatooine underworld. His death removed one of the most influential criminals in the galaxy.
