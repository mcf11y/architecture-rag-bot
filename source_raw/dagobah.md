# Dagobah

Dagobah was a remote, swampy world shrouded in mist and thick with tangled vegetation, teeming with strange creatures and dense with life. Far from any major trade route, it was a forgotten place, ideal for those who wished never to be found.

The planet was strong with the Force, its living energy so pervasive that it clouded the senses and made it difficult to tell friend from foe. This quality made Dagobah a natural refuge for the Jedi Master Yoda, who fled there in exile after the fall of the Jedi Order and the rise of the Galactic Empire.

For years Yoda lived alone in a small hut among the swamps, hiding from the Emperor and awaiting the day when he might train a new generation. That day came when Luke Skywalker journeyed to Dagobah in search of the legendary master. There, amid the mud and mist, Luke learned to deepen his connection to the Force and to confront the temptations of the dark side.
