# Mace Windu

Mace Windu was a senior member of the Jedi Council and one of the most powerful Jedi of his era, second only to Yoda in authority within the Jedi Order. He was famed for his stern discipline, his mastery of a rare and aggressive lightsaber combat form, and his distinctive purple blade.

A general during the Clone Wars, Mace Windu led clone troopers of the Galactic Republic in numerous battles. He was among the first to suspect that a Sith Lord had infiltrated the highest levels of government. When he finally uncovered that Chancellor Palpatine was in fact Darth Sidious, he led a small group of Jedi to arrest him.

In the confrontation that followed, Mace Windu nearly defeated Palpatine, but he was betrayed by Anakin Skywalker, who was falling to the dark side. Disarmed and struck down by the Sith Lord's lightning, Mace Windu fell to his death. His defeat cleared the way for Palpatine to destroy the Jedi Order and declare the Galactic Empire.
