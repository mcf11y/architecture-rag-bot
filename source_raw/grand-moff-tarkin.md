# Grand Moff Tarkin

Grand Moff Tarkin was a ruthless and ambitious military governor who served as one of the highest-ranking commanders of the Galactic Empire. A cold strategist, he believed the galaxy could be ruled through fear alone, a philosophy that guided his command of the Death Star.

Tarkin was the mastermind behind the doctrine of ruling by terror, arguing that the mere threat of the Death Star would keep entire star systems in line. To demonstrate its power, he ordered the destruction of Alderaan, the peaceful home planet of Princess Leia, killing billions in an instant.

Working closely with Darth Vader, Tarkin interrogated Leia and oversaw the pursuit of the stolen Death Star plans. He dismissed the Rebel Alliance as a minor nuisance and refused to evacuate the battle station even as the Rebels launched their desperate assault. Tarkin was killed when Luke Skywalker destroyed the Death Star at the Battle of Yavin.
