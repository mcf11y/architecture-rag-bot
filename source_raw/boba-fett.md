# Boba Fett

Boba Fett was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy, renowned for his distinctive armor, jetpack, and cold professionalism. He was an unaltered clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, who raised him as a son on the ocean world of Kamino.

After the death of his father during the Clone Wars, Boba grew into a ruthless and highly skilled tracker who took on dangerous contracts across the galaxy. He worked frequently for the crime lord Jabba the Hutt and occasionally for the Galactic Empire itself.

Boba Fett's most famous contract came when Darth Vader hired him to capture Han Solo. After Han was frozen in carbonite on Bespin, Boba delivered the smuggler to Jabba the Hutt to collect the bounty. He guarded his prize on Tatooine until Luke Skywalker and his allies arrived to rescue Han, in a desert battle that scattered Jabba's forces.
