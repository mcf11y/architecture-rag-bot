# Han Solo

Han Solo was a smuggler, pilot, and reluctant hero of the Rebel Alliance. Together with his loyal Wookiee companion Chewbacca, he flew the fastest freighter in the galaxy, the Millennium Falcon, a battered ship famous for its illegal modifications and powerful hyperdrive.

Han first met Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi in a cantina on Tatooine, agreeing to smuggle them off the planet for a fee. Though he claimed to care only about money, he returned to help destroy the Death Star at the Battle of Yavin. Over time he became a general in the Rebellion and a devoted ally.

Pursued by the crime lord Jabba the Hutt over an unpaid debt, Han was frozen in carbonite and handed over to the bounty hunter Boba Fett on the orders of Darth Vader. He was later rescued by his friends. Han fell in love with Princess Leia, and the two married after the defeat of the Empire.
