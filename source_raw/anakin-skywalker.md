# Anakin Skywalker

Anakin Skywalker was a gifted pilot and Jedi Knight who later fell to the dark side and became the Sith Lord known as Darth Vader. Born into slavery on Tatooine, he was discovered as a child by Qui-Gon Jinn, who believed the boy was destined to bring balance to the Force.

Trained by Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin grew into one of the most powerful Jedi of the Galactic Republic and a hero of the Clone Wars. He secretly married Padmé Amidala, a senator from Naboo. Haunted by fear of loss and manipulated by Chancellor Palpatine, who was secretly Darth Sidious, Anakin turned against the Jedi Order.

As Darth Vader, he enforced the will of the Galactic Empire for decades, hunting down the surviving Jedi and crushing the Rebellion. Encased in a black life-support suit after a duel on Mustafar, he became a feared enforcer of the Emperor. In the end, he was redeemed by his son, Luke Skywalker, and destroyed his Sith master.
