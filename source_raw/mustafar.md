# Mustafar

Mustafar was a hellish volcanic world of molten lava rivers, blackened rock, and constant eruptions. Its fiery surface, wreathed in smoke and ash, made it one of the most inhospitable planets in the galaxy, valued only for the rare minerals mined from its lava flows.

The planet became infamous as the site of a devastating duel between Obi-Wan Kenobi and his former apprentice Anakin Skywalker, who had fallen to the dark side and become Darth Vader. Amid the flames, the two former friends fought a brutal battle that ended with Anakin gravely wounded and burned on the shores of a lava river.

It was on Mustafar that Anakin Skywalker's transformation into the armored Sith Lord was sealed, for his injuries left him dependent on a black life-support suit for the rest of his life. In later years, Darth Vader kept a personal fortress on the volcanic world, a brooding monument to his fall and his devotion to the Emperor.
