# Order 66

Order 66 was a secret directive that commanded all clone troopers to turn on their Jedi commanders and execute them as traitors to the Galactic Republic. It was one of the most devastating betrayals in galactic history and marked the near-extinction of the Jedi Order.

The order had been embedded deep within the programming of the clone troopers from the moment they were grown on Kamino, a hidden contingency planted by the Sith Lord Darth Sidious. For years the clones served loyally beside their Jedi generals, unaware of the command waiting to be triggered.

When Palpatine gave the signal at the climax of the Clone Wars, clone troopers across the galaxy simultaneously turned their weapons on the Jedi they had fought alongside. Thousands of Jedi were slain in a single coordinated purge. Only a few, including Yoda and Obi-Wan Kenobi, escaped into hiding. With the Jedi shattered, Palpatine declared himself Emperor and founded the Galactic Empire.
