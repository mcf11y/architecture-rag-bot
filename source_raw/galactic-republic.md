# The Galactic Republic

The Galactic Republic was a democratic union of star systems that governed much of the galaxy for a thousand generations. Guided by the Galactic Senate and protected by the Jedi Order, it was long regarded as a symbol of civilization, cooperation, and peace.

The Republic was led by a Supreme Chancellor elected from among the senators who represented worlds across the galaxy. For centuries it maintained no standing army, relying on the Jedi to keep the peace. This changed with the outbreak of the Clone Wars, when the Republic raised a massive army of clone troopers to fight the Separatists.

In its final years the Republic grew corrupt and paralyzed by bureaucracy, making it vulnerable to manipulation. The senator Palpatine, secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, exploited the Clone Wars to seize emergency powers. When the conflict ended, he transformed the ailing Republic into the tyrannical Galactic Empire, ending a thousand years of democracy.
