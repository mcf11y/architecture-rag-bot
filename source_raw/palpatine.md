# Palpatine

Sheev Palpatine was a cunning politician who rose from senator of Naboo to Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Republic, and finally to Emperor of the Galactic Empire. In secret, he was Darth Sidious, a Sith Lord and master manipulator who orchestrated galactic war for his own gain.

Palpatine engineered the conflict between the Trade Federation, the Separatists, and the Republic, using the Clone Wars to accumulate emergency powers. He secretly commanded both sides of the war. When the moment was right, he issued Order 66, turning the clone troopers against their Jedi generals and nearly wiping out the Jedi Order.

Declaring himself Emperor, Palpatine ruled the galaxy through fear, backed by his apprentice Darth Vader and the might of the Death Star. He sought to turn Luke Skywalker to the dark side, but was ultimately betrayed and destroyed by Vader during the Battle of Endor. He was among the most powerful users of the dark side of the Force in history.
