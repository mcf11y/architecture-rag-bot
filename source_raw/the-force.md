# The Force

The Force was a mysterious energy field that bound the galaxy together, flowing through all living things and connecting them to one another. Those sensitive to it could learn to sense distant events, move objects with their minds, influence weaker wills, and perform feats far beyond ordinary ability.

The Force was said to have two aspects. The light side was associated with peace, knowledge, compassion, and self-discipline, and was followed by the Jedi. The dark side was fueled by fear, anger, hatred, and the hunger for power, and was embraced by the Sith. The eternal struggle between these two paths shaped the history of the galaxy.

Those born with an unusually strong connection to the Force were often identified as children and trained by the Jedi Order. Legends spoke of a chosen one who would bring balance to the Force by destroying the Sith. Anakin Skywalker was believed to be that prophesied figure, though the path to balance proved far darker and more painful than anyone foresaw.
