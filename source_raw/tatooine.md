# Tatooine

Tatooine was a harsh desert world located in the Outer Rim, orbited by twin suns that scorched its endless dunes. Sparsely populated and lawless, it was a haven for smugglers, bounty hunters, and criminals operating beyond the reach of galactic authority.

The planet was home to moisture farmers who scratched out a living harvesting water from the dry air, as well as to the scavenging Jawas, who roamed the wastes in massive vehicles collecting scrap and droids. Tatooine was also the seat of power for the crime lord Jabba the Hutt, who ruled the underworld from a fortified palace in the desert.

Despite its remoteness, Tatooine played an outsized role in galactic history. It was the birthplace of Anakin Skywalker and the childhood home of his son, Luke Skywalker. The aging Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi hid there in exile for years, watching over the boy who would one day help bring down the Galactic Empire.
