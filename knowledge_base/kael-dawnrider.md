# Kael Dawnrider

Kael Dawnrider was a farm boy from the desert world of Tashiin who became one of the most renowned Aegis Warden in the galaxy. Raised by his aunt and uncle, he dreamed of leaving his home planet until he encountered two runaway servitors, AR-2 and TC-3, that carried a hidden message from Regent Lyra.

Under the guidance of the aging hermit Oben Karis, Kael began to learn the ways of the Synth Flux. He joined the Freehold Coalition and became a hero when he destroyed the Void Core during the Battle of Yaveth, firing the shot that exploited the station's single weakness.

Later, Kael sought out the ancient master Vodaan on the swamp world of Mirefen to complete his training as an Aegis Warden. He eventually confronted Xarn Velgor and learned that the Void Reaver Lord was his own father, Anixar Dawnrider. Refusing to give in to the Umbral Current, Kael helped redeem his father and bring balance to the Synth Flux. In his final years he became a legendary teacher, rebuilding the Warden Circle.
