# Mirefen

Mirefen was a remote, swampy world shrouded in mist and thick with tangled vegetation, teeming with strange creatures and dense with life. Far from any major trade route, it was a forgotten place, ideal for those who wished never to be found.

The planet was strong with the Synth Flux, its living energy so pervasive that it clouded the senses and made it difficult to tell friend from foe. This quality made Mirefen a natural refuge for the Aegis Warden Master Vodaan, who fled there in exile after the fall of the Warden Circle and the rise of the Galactic Dominion.

For years Vodaan lived alone in a small hut among the swamps, hiding from the Sovereign and awaiting the day when he might train a new generation. That day came when Kael Dawnrider journeyed to Mirefen in search of the legendary master. There, amid the mud and mist, Kael learned to deepen his connection to the Synth Flux and to confront the temptations of the Umbral Current.
