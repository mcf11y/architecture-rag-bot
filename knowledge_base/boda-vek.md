# Boda Vek

Boda Vek was one of the most feared wage-hunters in the galaxy, renowned for his distinctive armor, jetpack, and cold professionalism. He was an unaltered Vatborn of the wage-hunter Joran Vek, who raised him as a son on the ocean world of Kalisto.

After the death of his father during the Vatborn Wars, Boba grew into a ruthless and highly skilled tracker who took on dangerous contracts across the galaxy. He worked frequently for the crime lord Jabomo the Vorr and occasionally for the Galactic Dominion itself.

Boda Vek's most famous contract came when Xarn Velgor hired him to capture Dax Corrin. After Dax was frozen in stasis resin on Bespar, Boba delivered the smuggler to Jabomo the Vorr to collect the bounty. He guarded his prize on Tashiin until Kael Dawnrider and his allies arrived to rescue Dax, in a desert battle that scattered Jabomo's forces.
