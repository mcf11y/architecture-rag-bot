# Groveth

Groveth was a lush forest world dominated by colossal wroshyr trees that towered kilometers into the sky, their vast branches supporting entire cities. It was the lush and verdant home planet of the Grovak, a species of tall, powerful, fur-covered warriors.

The Grovak of Groveth were renowned for their strength, loyalty, and skill as engineers and climbers. They lived in elevated cities built among the great trees, high above the dangerous creatures that prowled the forest floor. Their society valued honor and the sacred bond of the life debt.

During the Vatborn Wars, Groveth was the site of a major battle, defended by Grovak warriors alongside Vatborn legionaries and the Aegis Warden Master Vodaan. Later, under the Galactic Dominion, the planet suffered greatly as many Grovak were enslaved and forced into labor. The most famous Grovak, Grawlox, hailed from Groveth before joining Dax Corrin aboard the Void Kestrel.
