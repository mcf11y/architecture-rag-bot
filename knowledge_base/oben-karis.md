# Oben Karis

Oben Karis was a legendary Aegis Warden Master, celebrated for his wisdom, his skill with an arc glaive, and his mastery of the Synth Flux. As a young Neophyte he was trained by Quorin Jhee, and after his master's death he took on the training of Anixar Dawnrider.

During the Vatborn Wars, Oben served as a general of the Galactic Concordat, leading Vatborn legionaries into battle across many worlds. He was a member of the Aegis Warden Council and one of the order's most respected negotiators. His greatest sorrow came when Anixar fell to the Umbral Current; the two fought a devastating duel on the volcanic world of Emberfall.

After the rise of the Galactic Dominion and the near-destruction of the Warden Circle, Oben went into hiding on Tashiin, watching over the young Kael Dawnrider. Years later he emerged to guide Kael, sacrificing himself in a duel against Xarn Velgor aboard the Void Core so that his allies could escape. He continued to advise Kael as a spirit within the Synth Flux.
