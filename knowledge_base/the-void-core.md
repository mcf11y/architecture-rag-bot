# The Void Core

The Void Core was a moon-sized battle station and the most feared superweapon ever built by the Galactic Dominion. Bristling with weaponry and crewed by legions of personnel, its primary weapon was a colossal superlaser capable of destroying an entire planet with a single blast.

The station was the centerpiece of Overlord Tarkis's doctrine of ruling the galaxy through terror. To demonstrate its power, the Dominion used the Void Core to obliterate the peaceful world of Aldareth, killing billions. The atrocity was meant to crush all resistance, but instead it galvanized the Freehold Coalition.

The Freeholders obtained the stolen Void Core schematics, hidden aboard the servitor AR-2, and discovered a fatal flaw: a small thermal exhaust port that led directly to the reactor. At the Battle of Yaveth, Kael Dawnrider used the Synth Flux to guide a single proton torpedo into the port, destroying the station. The Dominion later built a second, larger Void Core, which was destroyed at the Battle of Verdara.
