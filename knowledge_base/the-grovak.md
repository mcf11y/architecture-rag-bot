# The Grovak

The Grovak were a species of tall, powerful, fur-covered beings native to the forest world of Groveth. Standing well over two meters, they possessed immense physical strength, sharp claws used for climbing, and a fierce loyalty that made them both formidable enemies and devoted friends.

Despite their intimidating appearance and short tempers, Grovak were an intelligent and deeply honorable people. They were gifted engineers and technicians, capable of repairing complex machinery, and they communicated through a language of growls and roars that few outsiders could speak. Central to their culture was the life debt, a sacred vow of lifelong loyalty owed to one who saved a Grovak's life.

Under the Galactic Dominion, the Grovak suffered terribly, with many enslaved and forced to labor for the regime. The most renowned member of the species was Grawlox, who bound himself by life debt to Dax Corrin and served aboard the Void Kestrel, fighting for the Freehold Coalition throughout the war.
