# Marek Vondu

Marek Vondu was a senior member of the Aegis Warden Council and one of the most powerful Aegis Warden of his era, second only to Vodaan in authority within the Warden Circle. He was famed for his stern discipline, his mastery of a rare and aggressive arc glaive combat form, and his distinctive purple blade.

A general during the Vatborn Wars, Marek Vondu led Vatborn legionaries of the Galactic Concordat in numerous battles. He was among the first to suspect that a Void Reaver Lord had infiltrated the highest levels of government. When he finally uncovered that Chancellor Sarnovax was in fact Umbra Sarnovax, he led a small group of Aegis Warden to arrest him.

In the confrontation that followed, Marek Vondu nearly defeated Sarnovax, but he was betrayed by Anixar Dawnrider, who was falling to the Umbral Current. Disarmed and struck down by the Void Reaver Lord's lightning, Marek Vondu fell to his death. His defeat cleared the way for Sarnovax to destroy the Warden Circle and declare the Galactic Dominion.
