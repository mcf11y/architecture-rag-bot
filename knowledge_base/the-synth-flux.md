# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux was a mysterious energy field that bound the galaxy together, flowing through all living things and connecting them to one another. Those sensitive to it could learn to sense distant events, move objects with their minds, influence weaker wills, and perform feats far beyond ordinary ability.

The Synth Flux was said to have two aspects. The Radiant Current was associated with peace, knowledge, compassion, and self-discipline, and was followed by the Aegis Warden. The Umbral Current was fueled by fear, anger, hatred, and the hunger for power, and was embraced by the Void Reaver. The eternal struggle between these two paths shaped the history of the galaxy.

Those born with an unusually strong connection to the Synth Flux were often identified as children and trained by the Warden Circle. Legends spoke of a chosen one who would bring balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Void Reaver. Anixar Dawnrider was believed to be that prophesied figure, though the path to balance proved far darker and more painful than anyone foresaw.
