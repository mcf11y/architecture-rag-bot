# Nabaris

Nabaris was a lush and beautiful world of rolling green hills, shimmering lakes, and elegant classical cities. It was home to two societies that shared the planet: the human inhabitants who lived on the surface, and the amphibious Bantuu who dwelt in underwater cities beneath its lakes.

Nabaris was the home planet of Padrienne Amalie, who served as its elected Queen and later as its senator, and also of Sarnovax Vael, who represented it in the the Conclave before rising to become Sovereign. The planet was known for its peaceful traditions and its skilled artisans.

Nabaris suffered a major crisis when the Mercantile Union blockaded and invaded it in a dispute over taxation and trade. The young Queen Amalie forged an alliance with the Bantuu and, with the help of the Aegis Warden, drove out the invaders. This conflict marked the return of the Void Reaver and the first steps in Sarnovax's long rise to absolute power.
