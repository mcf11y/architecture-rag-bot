# Padrienne Amalie

Padrienne Amalie was a courageous stateswoman who served as Queen and later senator of Nabaris. Elected to the throne as a young woman, she defended her home planet during a blockade and invasion by the Mercantile Union, forming an alliance with the native Bantuu to reclaim her capital.

As a senator in the the Conclave, Padrienne was a leading voice against corruption and against granting unchecked emergency powers to Chancellor Sarnovax during the Vatborn Wars. She secretly married the Aegis Warden Anixar Dawnrider, a forbidden union that she kept hidden from the Warden Circle.

Padrienne became pregnant with twins, but her marriage suffered as Anixar was drawn toward the Umbral Current of the Synth Flux. Heartbroken by his transformation into Xarn Velgor, she died shortly after giving birth to Kael Dawnrider and Lyra Solenne. Her children would grow up to become central figures in the Freehold Coalition and the fall of the Galactic Dominion.
