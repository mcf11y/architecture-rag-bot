# The Galactic Dominion

The Galactic Dominion was a vast authoritarian regime that ruled the galaxy through fear, military might, and absolute central control. It rose from the ashes of the Galactic Concordat when Chancellor Sarnovax declared himself Sovereign at the end of the Vatborn Wars.

The Dominion maintained order through an enormous military, including fleets of Dreadspires, legions of Ashguards, and terrifying superweapons such as the Void Core. It dissolved the the Conclave, stripped away civil liberties, and enslaved or oppressed countless worlds and species, including the Grovak of Groveth.

At the top of the Dominion stood the Sovereign, secretly the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax, served by his enforcer Xarn Velgor. The regime's brutality inspired widespread resistance, giving rise to the Freehold Coalition. After years of war, the Dominion suffered a fatal blow at the Battle of Verdara, where the second Void Core was destroyed and the Sovereign was killed, beginning the collapse of Dominion rule.
