# Dax Corrin

Dax Corrin was a smuggler, pilot, and reluctant hero of the Freehold Coalition. Together with his loyal Grovak companion Grawlox, he flew the fastest freighter in the galaxy, the Void Kestrel, a battered ship famous for its illegal modifications and powerful riftdrive.

Dax first met Kael Dawnrider and Oben Karis in a cantina on Tashiin, agreeing to smuggle them off the planet for a fee. Though he claimed to care only about money, he returned to help destroy the Void Core at the Battle of Yaveth. Over time he became a general in the Freehold Uprising and a devoted ally.

Pursued by the crime lord Jabomo the Vorr over an unpaid debt, Dax was frozen in stasis resin and handed over to the wage-hunter Boda Vek on the orders of Xarn Velgor. He was later rescued by his friends. Dax fell in love with Regent Lyra, and the two married after the defeat of the Dominion.
