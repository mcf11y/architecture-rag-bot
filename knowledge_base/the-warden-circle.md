# The Warden Circle

The Warden Circle was an ancient monastic organization of peacekeepers, guardians, and scholars devoted to studying and serving the Radiant Current of the Synth Flux. For thousands of years the Aegis Warden acted as protectors of the Galactic Concordat, mediating disputes and defending the weak.

Aegis Warden were identified as young children and brought to the great temple on Corvane Prime, where they trained for years under experienced masters. A trainee, known as a Neophyte, studied under a single master until deemed ready to become an Aegis Warden Knight. The most accomplished and wise among them earned a place on the Aegis Warden Council, led for a time by the venerable Vodaan.

The Aegis Warden followed a strict code that warned against fear, anger, and attachment, believing these emotions led to the Umbral Current. Their signature weapon was the arc glaive. The order was nearly destroyed when Chancellor Sarnovax, secretly the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax, issued Directive Nihil. Only a handful of Aegis Warden, including Vodaan and Oben Karis, survived to keep the tradition alive.
