# The Void Kestrel

The Void Kestrel was a battered but legendary freighter, widely considered the fastest ship of its kind in the galaxy. Though it looked like a worn-out piece of junk from the outside, it had been heavily and illegally modified to outrun almost anything that pursued it.

The ship was famous for its lightning-quick riftdrive, its powerful engines, and its ability to slip away from danger at the last possible moment. It was originally won and lost in games of chance, passing from Rennic Calvane into the hands of the smuggler Dax Corrin, who flew it with his Grovak co-pilot Grawlox.

The Void Kestrel played a decisive role in the war against the Galactic Dominion. It carried Kael Dawnrider, Oben Karis, and Regent Lyra on their earliest adventures, and it flew in the Battle of Yaveth. At the Battle of Verdara, Rennic Calvane piloted the Falcon into the heart of the second Void Core and fired the shot that destroyed its reactor.
