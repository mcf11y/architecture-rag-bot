# Vodaan

Vodaan was one of the oldest and wisest members of the Warden Circle, serving as Grand Master of the Aegis Warden Council for centuries. Small in stature but immensely powerful in the Synth Flux, he trained countless Aegis Warden over the course of nearly nine hundred years.

Known for his unusual manner of speech and his deep humility, Vodaan was a formidable warrior despite his age, wielding a short arc glaive with astonishing agility. During the Vatborn Wars he commanded armies of Vatborn legionaries on behalf of the Galactic Concordat. When the Warden Circle was betrayed and destroyed under Directive Nihil, Vodaan survived and went into exile.

He hid on the remote swamp world of Mirefen, concealing himself from the Galactic Dominion and the Sovereign, Umbra Sarnovax. There he later trained Kael Dawnrider in the ways of the Synth Flux, teaching him to resist the temptations of the Umbral Current. Vodaan died peacefully of old age, becoming one with the Synth Flux.
