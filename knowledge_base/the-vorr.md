# The Vorr

The Vorr were a species of large, sluglike beings notorious throughout the galaxy for their dominance of organized crime. Slow-moving and long-lived, they possessed no legs, dragging their massive bodies along the ground, yet they wielded enormous influence through wealth, intimidation, and vast criminal networks.

Vorr were cunning, greedy, and utterly without mercy, ruling over empires of smuggling, gambling, slavery, and bounty hunting. They cared little for the wars between the Galactic Concordat, the Secessionists, or the Galactic Dominion, concerned only with profit and the expansion of their own power. Many operated from strongholds in the lawless Outer Rim.

The most infamous Vorr was Jabomo the Vorr, a powerful crime lord who ruled the underworld of Tashiin from a fortified desert palace. He employed smugglers such as Dax Corrin and wage-hunters such as Boda Vek, and displayed the stasis resin-frozen Dax as a trophy until Kael Dawnrider and his allies destroyed the crime lord's operation.
