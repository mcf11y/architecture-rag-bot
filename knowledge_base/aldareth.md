# Aldareth

Aldareth was a serene and beautiful world known throughout the galaxy for its peaceful culture, its commitment to art and diplomacy, and its rejection of war. Ruled by a royal family, it was widely regarded as one of the most civilized planets in the galaxy.

The planet was the adopted home of Regent Lyra Solenne, whose royal family were secret supporters of the Freehold Coalition. Aldareth maintained no significant military forces, priding itself on its pacifist traditions, and its leaders were respected voices in the the Conclave.

Aldareth met a tragic and infamous end when Overlord Tarkis ordered the Void Core to destroy it as a demonstration of the battle station's power. The entire planet and its billions of inhabitants were annihilated in a single blast, an atrocity meant to intimidate the galaxy into submission. Instead, the destruction of Aldareth became a rallying cry that fueled the Freehold Uprising against the Galactic Dominion.
