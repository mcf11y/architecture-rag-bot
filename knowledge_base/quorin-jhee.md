# Quorin Jhee

Quorin Jhee was a wise and independent-minded Aegis Warden Master known for trusting his instincts and following what he called the living Synth Flux. Though highly respected, he often clashed with the Aegis Warden Council, preferring to act on his own judgment rather than strict tradition.

Quorin was the master of Oben Karis, whom he trained into a capable Aegis Warden Knight. While on a mission to protect Padrienne Amalie during the Mercantile Union's blockade of Nabaris, Quorin discovered a gifted young slave on Tashiin named Anixar Dawnrider. Convinced that the boy was the chosen one destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux, he insisted the Warden Circle train him.

Quorin was slain in a duel by the Void Reaver apprentice Korth Maal on Nabaris, becoming one of the first casualties in the resurgence of the Void Reaver. With his dying words he asked Oben to train Anixar. Quorin later became one of the first Aegis Warden to learn how to preserve his consciousness within the Synth Flux after death.
