# Jabomo the Vorr

Jabomo the Vorr was a powerful crime lord who ruled a vast criminal empire from his palace on the desert world of Tashiin. A member of the sluglike Vorr species, he was enormous, slow-moving, and utterly ruthless, controlling smuggling, gambling, and bounty hunting across the Outer Rim.

Jabomo employed a wide network of smugglers, including Dax Corrin, who owed him a large debt after being forced to dump a shipment of cargo. When Dax failed to pay, Jabomo placed a bounty on his head, which was eventually collected by the wage-hunter Boda Vek. Jabomo displayed the stasis resin-frozen smuggler as a trophy in his throne room.

The crime lord met his end when Kael Dawnrider, Regent Lyra, and their allies infiltrated his palace to rescue Dax Corrin. During the escape, Lyra strangled Jabomo, ending his reign over the Tashiin underworld. His death removed one of the most influential criminals in the galaxy.
