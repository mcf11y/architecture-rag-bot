# The Vatborn Wars

The Vatborn Wars were a galaxy-spanning conflict fought between the Galactic Concordat and the Secessionists, a confederacy of worlds and corporations that sought to break away from central rule. The war raged for years across countless planets and reshaped the galaxy forever.

The Concordat fought with a vast army of Vatborn legionaries, grown on the ocean world of Kalisto and led into battle by Aegis Warden generals such as Oben Karis, Anixar Dawnrider, Marek Vondu, and Vodaan. The Secessionists, backed by the Mercantile Union, fielded enormous armies of battle servitors. Neither side realized that the entire war had been secretly engineered by the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax.

The Vatborn Wars served as the perfect cover for Sarnovax's rise to power. As the fighting dragged on, he accumulated emergency authority, eroded the Warden Circle's influence, and prepared his final move. The war ended when he issued Directive Nihil, turning the Vatborn legionaries against the Aegis Warden and clearing the path for the birth of the Galactic Dominion.
