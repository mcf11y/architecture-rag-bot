# TC-3

TC-3 was a protocol servitor fluent in over six million forms of communication, built to assist with etiquette, translation, and diplomacy. Golden-plated and perpetually anxious, he was known for his fussy manners and his constant worrying, which often provided comic relief amid danger.

TC-3 was originally constructed by a young Anixar Dawnrider on Tashiin. Over the years the servitor passed through many owners, eventually serving Padrienne Amalie and later the Freehold Coalition. He was almost always accompanied by his counterpart, the astromech servitor AR-2, with whom he shared countless adventures.

Though timid and easily flustered, TC-3 proved surprisingly important to the Freehold Uprising. On the forest moon of Verdara, the native Brindles mistook the golden servitor for a deity, and TC-3 persuaded them to join the fight against the Galactic Dominion. His fluency in language repeatedly helped his friends survive hostile encounters across the galaxy.
