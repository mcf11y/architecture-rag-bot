# Verdara

Verdara was a forested moon covered in towering ancient trees, gentle meadows, and abundant wildlife. Its temperate climate and lush greenery made it a haven for life, including its native inhabitants, the small furry Brindles who lived in villages built high among the treetops.

The moon gained galactic significance when the Galactic Dominion chose to construct a second, far larger Void Core in orbit above it. To protect the unfinished battle station, the Dominion built a powerful shield generator on the moon's surface, defended by a garrison of Ashguards.

Verdara became the site of the decisive Battle of Verdara. A Freehold Coalition strike team, aided by the Brindles, landed on the moon to destroy the shield generator, while the Freeholder fleet engaged the Dominion in orbit. The primitive but determined Brindles helped defeat the Dominion forces on the ground, allowing the Freeholders to destroy the second Void Core and the Sovereign along with it.
