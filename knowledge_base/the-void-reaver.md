# The Void Reaver

The Void Reaver were an ancient order of Synth Flux-users who embraced the Umbral Current and stood as the eternal enemies of the Aegis Warden. Driven by ambition, hatred, and the desire for power, the Void Reaver sought to dominate the galaxy and destroy the Warden Circle that opposed them.

For much of their later history, the Void Reaver followed a strict rule that allowed only two to exist at any time: a master to embody power, and an apprentice to crave it. This rule of two kept their numbers hidden and their ambitions secret for a thousand years, as they patiently plotted revenge against the Aegis Warden.

The Void Reaver achieved their greatest triumph under Umbra Sarnovax, who concealed his identity as the politician Sarnovax. Working with apprentices such as Korth Maal and later Xarn Velgor, he orchestrated the Vatborn Wars, destroyed the Warden Circle through Directive Nihil, and founded the Galactic Dominion. The Void Reaver wielded arc glaives, typically with red blades, and drew their strength from raw emotion and the Umbral Current of the Synth Flux.
