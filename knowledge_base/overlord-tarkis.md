# Overlord Tarkis

Overlord Tarkis was a ruthless and ambitious military governor who served as one of the highest-ranking commanders of the Galactic Dominion. A cold strategist, he believed the galaxy could be ruled through fear alone, a philosophy that guided his command of the Void Core.

Tarkis was the mastermind behind the doctrine of ruling by terror, arguing that the mere threat of the Void Core would keep entire star systems in line. To demonstrate its power, he ordered the destruction of Aldareth, the peaceful home planet of Regent Lyra, killing billions in an instant.

Working closely with Xarn Velgor, Tarkis interrogated Lyra and oversaw the pursuit of the stolen Void Core schematics. He dismissed the Freehold Coalition as a minor nuisance and refused to evacuate the battle station even as the Freeholders launched their desperate assault. Tarkis was killed when Kael Dawnrider destroyed the Void Core at the Battle of Yaveth.
