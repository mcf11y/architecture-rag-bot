# The Dreadspire

The Dreadspire was a massive wedge-shaped warship that served as the primary symbol of the Galactic Dominion's military might. Kilometers long and bristling with turbolasers, it could dominate entire star systems and strike terror into any world that considered defying Dominion rule.

Each Dreadspire carried thousands of crew, legions of Ashguards, squadrons of Wraith fighters, and ground assault vehicles, allowing it to project power both in space and on planetary surfaces. Its sheer size and firepower made it a mobile fortress capable of blockading planets and crushing resistance.

Fleets of Dreadspires enforced the Sovereign's will across the galaxy, hunting the Freehold Coalition from world to world. It was a Dreadspire that first captured Regent Lyra's ship as she fled with the stolen Void Core schematics. These warships remained the iron backbone of the Dominion until its defeat at the Battle of Verdara and the long collapse that followed.
