# Anixar Dawnrider

Anixar Dawnrider was a gifted pilot and Aegis Warden Knight who later fell to the Umbral Current and became the Void Reaver Lord known as Xarn Velgor. Born into slavery on Tashiin, he was discovered as a child by Quorin Jhee, who believed the boy was destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux.

Trained by Oben Karis, Anixar grew into one of the most powerful Aegis Warden of the Galactic Concordat and a hero of the Vatborn Wars. He secretly married Padrienne Amalie, a senator from Nabaris. Haunted by fear of loss and manipulated by Chancellor Sarnovax, who was secretly Umbra Sarnovax, Anixar turned against the Warden Circle.

As Xarn Velgor, he enforced the will of the Galactic Dominion for decades, hunting down the surviving Aegis Warden and crushing the Freehold Uprising. Encased in a black life-support suit after a duel on Emberfall, he became a feared enforcer of the Sovereign. In the end, he was redeemed by his son, Kael Dawnrider, and destroyed his Void Reaver master.
