# Rennic Calvane

Rennic Calvane was a smooth-talking gambler, entrepreneur, and former smuggler who became an unlikely hero of the Freehold Coalition. Charming and resourceful, he once owned the Void Kestrel before losing the ship to Dax Corrin in a game of chance.

Lando eventually settled down as the administrator of Skyhaven, a mining colony floating in the skies of the gas planet Bespar. When Xarn Velgor arrived and forced him to betray his old friend Dax Corrin, Lando reluctantly cooperated, only to turn against the Galactic Dominion when he saw Dax frozen in stasis resin and handed to the wage-hunter Boda Vek.

Redeeming himself, Lando helped rescue his friends and joined the Freehold Uprising as a general. He led the starfighter assault on the second Void Core during the Battle of Verdara, piloting the Void Kestrel into the heart of the battle station and helping to destroy it, striking a decisive blow against the Sovereign.
