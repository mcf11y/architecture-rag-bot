# Grawlox

Grawlox was a Grovak warrior from the forest world of Groveth and the loyal co-pilot and closest friend of Dax Corrin. Standing more than two meters tall and covered in shaggy brown fur, he was immensely strong, fiercely loyal, and skilled with a bowcaster.

Grawlox owed Dax a life debt, a sacred Grovak bond that kept the two companions together for decades aboard the Void Kestrel. Though he spoke only in growls and roars that few outsiders could understand, Grawl was a brilliant mechanic who kept the ship's aging riftdrive running through countless narrow escapes.

He fought alongside the Freehold Coalition in many battles, including the Battle of Verdara, where the Grovak helped the Freeholders bring down the shield protecting the second Void Core. Grawlox remained at Dax Corrin's side through smuggling runs, wars, and adventures, a symbol of unwavering friendship and courage.
