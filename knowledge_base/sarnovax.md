# Sarnovax

Sarnovax Vael was a cunning politician who rose from senator of Nabaris to High Chancellor of the Galactic Concordat, and finally to Sovereign of the Galactic Dominion. In secret, he was Umbra Sarnovax, a Void Reaver Lord and master manipulator who orchestrated galactic war for his own gain.

Sarnovax engineered the conflict between the Mercantile Union, the Secessionists, and the Concordat, using the Vatborn Wars to accumulate emergency powers. He secretly commanded both sides of the war. When the moment was right, he issued Directive Nihil, turning the Vatborn legionaries against their Aegis Warden generals and nearly wiping out the Warden Circle.

Declaring himself Sovereign, Sarnovax ruled the galaxy through fear, backed by his apprentice Xarn Velgor and the might of the Void Core. He sought to turn Kael Dawnrider to the Umbral Current, but was ultimately betrayed and destroyed by Xarn during the Battle of Verdara. He was among the most powerful users of the Umbral Current of the Synth Flux in history.
