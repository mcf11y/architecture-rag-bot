# Kalisto

Kalisto was a remote ocean world beyond the edge of the known galaxy, its surface entirely covered by vast, storm-swept seas. Its cities were built on tall stilts rising above the endless waves, and its tall, graceful inhabitants were masters of an advanced science: cloning.

The Kaminoans were the finest cloners in the galaxy, and it was on this hidden world that a massive secret army was grown. Using the wage-hunter Joran Vek as a genetic template, they produced legions of Vatborn legionaries, bred and trained from birth to serve as soldiers.

This Vatborn army was secretly commissioned in the name of the Galactic Concordat, though the true architect of the scheme was the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax. The Vatborn formed the backbone of the Concordat's forces during the Vatborn Wars, and were later turned against their own Aegis Warden commanders through Directive Nihil, becoming the first soldiers of the Galactic Dominion.
