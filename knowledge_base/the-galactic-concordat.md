# The Galactic Concordat

The Galactic Concordat was a democratic union of star systems that governed much of the galaxy for a thousand generations. Guided by the the Conclave and protected by the Warden Circle, it was long regarded as a symbol of civilization, cooperation, and peace.

The Concordat was led by a High Chancellor elected from among the senators who represented worlds across the galaxy. For centuries it maintained no standing army, relying on the Aegis Warden to keep the peace. This changed with the outbreak of the Vatborn Wars, when the Concordat raised a massive army of Vatborn legionaries to fight the Secessionists.

In its final years the Concordat grew corrupt and paralyzed by bureaucracy, making it vulnerable to manipulation. The senator Sarnovax, secretly the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax, exploited the Vatborn Wars to seize emergency powers. When the conflict ended, he transformed the ailing Concordat into the tyrannical Galactic Dominion, ending a thousand years of democracy.
