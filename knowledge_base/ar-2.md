# AR-2

AR-2 was a courageous and resourceful astromech servitor who played a pivotal role in the greatest events of the galaxy. Small, dome-topped, and communicating only in beeps and whistles, the servitor served loyally alongside heroes across several generations.

AR-2 accompanied Anixar Dawnrider as a fighter pilot's servitor during the Vatborn Wars, and later served Padrienne Amalie. The servitor was famously entrusted with the stolen Void Core schematics by Regent Lyra, carrying the schematics to Tashiin and setting in motion the events that led to the Freehold Coalition's first great victory.

Paired almost constantly with the protocol servitor TC-3, AR-2 repeatedly saved his companions through daring repairs, computer intrusions, and quick thinking. He served Kael Dawnrider as an Talon fighter astromech and remained a steadfast ally of the Freehold Uprising throughout the war against the Galactic Dominion. Few servitors ever influenced galactic history so profoundly.
