# Lyra Solenne

Regent Lyra Solenne was a political leader, diplomat, and one of the founding commanders of the Freehold Coalition. Raised as the adopted daughter of the royal family of Aldareth, she served in the the Conclave while secretly working against the Galactic Dominion.

Captured aboard a Dreadspire while carrying the stolen Void Core schematics, Lyra hid the schematics inside the servitor AR-2 before being taken prisoner by Xarn Velgor. She was rescued from the Void Core by Kael Dawnrider, Dax Corrin, and Grawlox. Her home planet of Aldareth was destroyed by the Void Core on the orders of Overlord Tarkis, a tragedy that hardened her resolve.

Lyra was a natural leader who commanded troops during the Battle of Glacem and the Battle of Verdara. She was later revealed to be the twin sister of Kael Dawnrider and the daughter of Padrienne Amalie and Anixar Dawnrider, making her strong in the Synth Flux. She married Dax Corrin after the fall of the Dominion.
