# Korth Maal

Korth Maal was a fearsome Void Reaver apprentice trained in secret by Umbra Sarnovax to help restore the power of the Void Reaver and destroy the Warden Circle. Marked by red and black tattoos across his entire body, he was a silent and relentless warrior driven by rage and the Umbral Current of the Synth Flux.

Maal's signature weapon was a double-bladed arc glaive, which he wielded with acrobatic ferocity. He was dispatched by his master to hunt down Padrienne Amalie and the Aegis Warden protecting her during the crisis on Nabaris. There he fought and killed Quorin Jhee in a fierce duel.

Moments later, Maal was struck down by Oben Karis and fell into a deep shaft, though he survived against all odds. Consumed by hatred and a thirst for revenge against Karis, he rebuilt himself and rose to power in the criminal underworld during the years of the Vatborn Wars. He remained one of the most dangerous enemies the Aegis Warden ever faced.
