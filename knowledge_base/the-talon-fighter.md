# The Talon fighter

The Talon fighter was a versatile single-pilot starfighter that became the backbone of the Freehold Coalition's fleet. Named for the distinctive way its four wings split into an X-shape during combat, it balanced speed, firepower, and durability better than almost any other fighter of its class.

Each Talon fighter carried powerful laser cannons and proton torpedoes, and was equipped with a socket for an astromech servitor, such as AR-2, that managed repairs and navigation in flight. Its riftdrive allowed pilots to travel between star systems, making it capable of both defense and long-range strikes.

The Talon fighter earned its place in history at the Battle of Yaveth, where a small squadron of the fighters attacked the Void Core. Flying an Talon fighter with AR-2 in the socket behind him, Kael Dawnrider trusted the Synth Flux to guide his aim and fired the shot that destroyed the battle station. The fighter went on to serve the Freehold Uprising in countless battles against the Galactic Dominion.
