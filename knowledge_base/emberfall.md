# Emberfall

Emberfall was a hellish volcanic world of molten lava rivers, blackened rock, and constant eruptions. Its fiery surface, wreathed in smoke and ash, made it one of the most inhospitable planets in the galaxy, valued only for the rare minerals mined from its lava flows.

The planet became infamous as the site of a devastating duel between Oben Karis and his former apprentice Anixar Dawnrider, who had fallen to the Umbral Current and become Xarn Velgor. Amid the flames, the two former friends fought a brutal battle that ended with Anixar gravely wounded and burned on the shores of a lava river.

It was on Emberfall that Anixar Dawnrider's transformation into the armored Void Reaver Lord was sealed, for his injuries left him dependent on a black life-support suit for the rest of his life. In later years, Xarn Velgor kept a personal fortress on the volcanic world, a brooding monument to his fall and his devotion to the Sovereign.
