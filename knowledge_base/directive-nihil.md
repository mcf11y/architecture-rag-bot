# Directive Nihil

Directive Nihil was a secret directive that commanded all Vatborn legionaries to turn on their Aegis Warden commanders and execute them as traitors to the Galactic Concordat. It was one of the most devastating betrayals in galactic history and marked the near-extinction of the Warden Circle.

The order had been embedded deep within the programming of the Vatborn legionaries from the moment they were grown on Kalisto, a hidden contingency planted by the Void Reaver Lord Umbra Sarnovax. For years the Vatborn served loyally beside their Aegis Warden generals, unaware of the command waiting to be triggered.

When Sarnovax gave the signal at the climax of the Vatborn Wars, Vatborn legionaries across the galaxy simultaneously turned their weapons on the Aegis Warden they had fought alongside. Thousands of Aegis Warden were slain in a single coordinated purge. Only a few, including Vodaan and Oben Karis, escaped into hiding. With the Aegis Warden shattered, Sarnovax declared himself Sovereign and founded the Galactic Dominion.
