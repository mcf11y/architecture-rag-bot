# Corvane Prime

Corvane Prime was a densely populated city-world at the very heart of the galaxy, its entire surface covered by towering skyscrapers and endless layers of urban sprawl. For thousands of years it served as the political capital, first of the Galactic Concordat and later of the Galactic Dominion.

The planet was home to the the Conclave, where representatives from countless star systems gathered to debate and govern. It also housed the great temple of the Warden Circle, the training ground and headquarters of the Aegis Warden for generations. Beneath its glittering upper levels lay a shadowy underworld of crime and poverty.

Corvane Prime was the stage for many pivotal events, including the political maneuvering of Chancellor Sarnovax as he transformed the Concordat into the Dominion. When Sarnovax issued Directive Nihil and declared himself Sovereign, it was on Corvane Prime that the new regime centered its power, ruling the galaxy from the same halls that had once been dedicated to democracy.
