# Tashiin

Tashiin was a harsh desert world located in the Outer Rim, orbited by twin suns that scorched its endless dunes. Sparsely populated and lawless, it was a haven for smugglers, wage-hunters, and criminals operating beyond the reach of galactic authority.

The planet was home to moisture farmers who scratched out a living harvesting water from the dry air, as well as to the scavenging Skree, who roamed the wastes in massive vehicles collecting scrap and servitors. Tashiin was also the seat of power for the crime lord Jabomo the Vorr, who ruled the underworld from a fortified palace in the desert.

Despite its remoteness, Tashiin played an outsized role in galactic history. It was the birthplace of Anixar Dawnrider and the childhood home of his son, Kael Dawnrider. The aging Aegis Warden Oben Karis hid there in exile for years, watching over the boy who would one day help bring down the Galactic Dominion.
