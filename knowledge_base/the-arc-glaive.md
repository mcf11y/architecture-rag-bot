# The Arc glaive

The arc glaive was the iconic weapon of both the Aegis Warden and the Void Reaver, a blade of pure energy projected from an elegant handheld hilt. Capable of cutting through nearly any material and of deflecting pulse-caster bolts, it was described as an elegant weapon for a more civilized age.

Constructing an arc glaive was a rite of passage for an Aegis Warden. At the heart of each weapon lay a special crystal that focused the energy blade and determined its color. Aegis Warden commonly wielded blue or green blades, while the Void Reaver favored red crystals, corrupted through the Umbral Current of the Synth Flux.

Mastery of the arc glaive required deep training and a strong connection to the Synth Flux, allowing a warrior to anticipate attacks and move with superhuman speed. Legendary duels defined galactic history, from Oben Karis's battle with Korth Maal, to his fateful duel with Anixar Dawnrider on Emberfall, to Kael Dawnrider's confrontation with Xarn Velgor. The weapon remained a symbol of the ancient orders that wielded it.
