# The Freehold Coalition

The Freehold Coalition was a coalition of freedom fighters, defectors, and oppressed worlds united in armed resistance against the Galactic Dominion. Formed in secret by senators and idealists who refused to accept tyranny, it fought to restore freedom and democracy to the galaxy.

Though vastly outnumbered and outgunned, the Freehold Uprising relied on daring raids, guerrilla tactics, and the courage of volunteers. Its heroes included Regent Lyra Solenne, the smuggler Dax Corrin, and the young Aegis Warden Kael Dawnrider, whose destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Yaveth gave the Alliance its first great victory.

The Freehold Coalition endured hardship and defeat, including the loss of its hidden base during the Battle of Glacem. Yet it persevered, striking its decisive blow at the Battle of Verdara, where Freeholder forces destroyed the second Void Core and killed the Sovereign. This triumph shattered the Dominion's grip on the galaxy and paved the way for a new era of freedom.
